# Cover artwork

The Coolify logo comes from [coollabsio/coolify, public/coolify-logo.svg](https://github.com/coollabsio/coolify/blob/main/public/coolify-logo.svg), distributed under the Apache License 2.0 (see [LICENSE](../LICENSE)). The logo paths and colors are unchanged; the integration cover adds a background and centers the logo.

`cover.svg` is the editable source of `cover.png`. Export at exactly 800 × 534 pixels, as an opaque PNG under 150 KB, to meet the Gladys cover requirements. Keep the PNG and SVG synchronized.
